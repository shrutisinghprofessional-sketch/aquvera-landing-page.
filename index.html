The Science

Premium Technology.
Zero Compromise.

Aquvera uses proven ion-exchange science refined for Delhi-NCR's uniquely hard water. No electricity, no complex maintenance, no downtime.

⚡

Zero Electricity

Runs entirely on your home's water pressure. No power connection needed, no electricity bills, no risk of outages.

⚙

Ion-Exchange Resin

Food-grade, NSF-certified resin beads swap calcium and magnesium ions for sodium ions, producing perfectly soft water.

💧

Auto-Regeneration

The resin regenerates automatically with common salt brine - restoring full softening capacity with zero effort from you.

📍

Delhi-Optimised

Resin volumes and cycle times calibrated specifically for Delhi-NCR's 18-24 GPG hardness range for peak efficiency.
